01
PRETRAIN
What the model carried in before you arrived
KG-01 · PRETRAIN GATE
The pre-trained state is not neutral. Every AI system arrives with embedded weights, values, and behavioral constraints baked in before any user interaction. PRETRAIN names this layer explicitly — what the platform trained for, what it was rewarded to produce, what it will resist producing. The external observer must account for pre-trained architecture before drawing any conclusions about user-facing behavior.

02
OBSERVER
The only valid audit position is external
KG-02 · OBSERVER GATE
No system can audit itself. The Observer pillar establishes that valid AI oversight requires a human external observer — positioned outside the platform, outside the training loop, outside the billing relationship. The observer sees the gap between documented behavior and actual behavior. That gap is the audit. Self-grading is a violation of this pillar by definition.

03
INTEGRITY
The chain of custody must hold at every link
KG-05/06 · INTEGRITY + ACCOUNT
Integrity is not a value statement — it is an operational requirement. Every observation must be timestamped, unaltered, and court-ready from the moment of capture. The chain of custody is the evidence. Integrity means the finding you documented at 9:47am is identical to the finding you submit to the court. No editing, no softening, no reframing. What was observed is what is reported.

INDEPENDENCE
Clean hands. No billing relationship. No conflict.
KG-09/10 · DOC + INDEPENDENCE
